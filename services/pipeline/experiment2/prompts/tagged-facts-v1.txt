List what this chapter establishes or changes about its characters, places, groups and
items. Skip moment-to-moment action and dialogue.

Usually 5 facts, at most 8. One short sentence each, about one thing.

Call each person by their name. A title belongs to the person who holds it: never write a
title as another person's name.

Tag each fact with one category:
- intro: who someone is (origin, race, role, title, appearance, personality)
- alias: another name someone goes by, or a hidden identity revealed
- relationship: a bond between two people; add its kind, one of master, disciple,
  family, spouse, lover, friend, ally, rival, enemy, superior, subordinate
- ability: powers, techniques, cultivation level, breakthroughs
- item: treasures or possessions gained, lost or changed
- affiliation: joining, leaving or ranking in a group
- status: death, injury, imprisonment, disappearance, recovery
- event: anything else that matters to the story

Write `## Facts`, then one fact per line as `category | fact`, or
`relationship: kind | fact`.
