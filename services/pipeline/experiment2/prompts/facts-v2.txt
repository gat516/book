You are reading one chapter of a web novel for a reader's companion guide.

Pick out the facts from this chapter that a reader would need to remember to follow
the rest of the story: who people are, what they can do, who is allied with or against
whom, what was gained or lost, promises and threats, and how the world works.

Skip moment-to-moment action unless it changes something that matters later.

Think it through however you like first. Then give your list under a heading
`## Facts`. A good shape for each line is:

- [category] Fact in plain English. (names involved)

Use whatever categories fit. If a fact doesn't fit that shape, write it the way that
makes sense. Keep predictions, plans and threats marked as such. Don't state them as
things that happened.
