You are reading one chapter of a web novel for a reader's companion guide.

Pick out the facts from this chapter that a reader would need to remember to follow
the rest of the story: who people are, what they can do, who is allied with or against
whom, what was gained or lost, why characters made key decisions, promises, threats,
unresolved loose ends, and how the world works.

Skip moment-to-moment action unless it changes something that matters later.

Think it through however you like first. Then write the heading `## Facts` and, below
it, one fact per line in this form:

category | status | names | fact

- category: one of character, relationship, faction, item, ability, world, plot.
  Use another single word only if none of these fit.
- status: happened, ongoing, plan, threat, or implied. Use "implied" when the chapter
  strongly suggests something without showing it. Never mark a plan, threat or
  implication as happened.
- names: everyone and everything the fact involves, separated by semicolons, spelled
  exactly as the chapter spells them.
- fact: one plain English sentence.

Example:
relationship | happened | Alice; Bob | Alice swore loyalty to Bob after he saved her life.

Nothing else goes below the heading: no bullets, bold, numbering or blank lines.
