You are reading one chapter of a web novel for a reader's companion guide.

Track the story threads in this chapter. A thread is a storyline that runs across
chapters: a conflict, rivalry, alliance, quest, grudge, promise, mystery, power-up path
or open question. For each thread this chapter touches, say how it developed here,
including why characters made the key decisions they did. Threads that are opened and
left hanging (curses, threats, invitations, grudges, unanswered questions) matter as
much as ones that are resolved.

Skip moment-to-moment action unless it moves a thread.

Think it through however you like first. Then write the heading `## Threads` and, below
it, one line per thread development in this form:

thread | status | names | development

- thread: a short name for the storyline that would still make sense in a later
  chapter, e.g. "Alice vs Bob" or "the missing sword".
- status: opened, advanced, or resolved. Only use resolved if the chapter shows the
  outcome; an outcome that is only expected or implied is advanced.
- names: the people, groups, places and named items involved, separated by
  semicolons and spelled exactly as the chapter spells them. No numbers or
  descriptions.
- development: one plain English sentence on what happened to the thread in this
  chapter and why.

Example:
Alice vs Bob | advanced | Alice; Bob; Crown of Ash | Alice stole the Crown of Ash from Bob because she believes he murdered her mentor.

Nothing else goes below the heading: no bullets, bold, numbering or blank lines.
