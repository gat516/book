Three days later, Ling Feng returned to the small town outside the Order Temple.

The townsfolk did not yet know that this young man now possessed the Chaos Star God Body. They only knew that he had killed Aries in the Twisting Star Path.

In the tavern, someone said in a low voice, "Lord Abaddon has made it known that he will avenge Aries no matter what."

Ling Feng sat in the corner and slowly finished a cup of wine. The Chaos Star God Body circulated within him, his one hundred and eight star veins more solid than three days before.

He knew very well that Abaddon would not wait long.
