In the depths of the Twisting Star Path, the starlight ebbed away like a tide.

Ling Feng opened his eyes as all one hundred and eight star veins in his body lit up at once. He had finally forged the legendary Chaos Star God Body.

Not far away, Aries lay on the ground, lifeless. He had ambushed Ling Feng to seize the Star God's legacy and was killed by a single palm strike instead.

When Abaddon, the Void God, arrived, he found only his disciple's corpse. He stared at Ling Feng and said, word by word, "I will take back Aries's life with my own hands."

Freya stepped in front of Ling Feng and said coolly, "Your disciple struck first. If you want revenge, you'll have to get through me."

Abaddon snorted and left.
