That night, a letter was delivered to Ling Feng.

It was from Freya: "Abaddon has gone to the Order Temple to seek an audience with Metatron. He wants to borrow the Order Temple's power to avenge Aries. They have their eyes on the Chaos Star God Body you hold, too."

Ling Feng burned the letter.

The next day, a wandering cultivator named "Long Fei" appeared in town. No one connected him with the man who had killed Aries.

Only Freya, catching sight of him in the crowd, smiled slightly and called out, "Long Fei."
