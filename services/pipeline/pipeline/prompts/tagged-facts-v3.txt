List the most important things this chapter establishes, reveals, or permanently changes about its characters, organizations, places, items, and world.

Focus on facts that are likely to matter in later chapters or that significantly update the reader's understanding of the story.

Skip moment-to-moment action, dialogue, temporary positions, minor observations, and details that matter only within the current scene.

Before including a fact, ask:

**Would a reader benefit from remembering this several chapters later?**

If not, leave it out.

Usually output 3–5 facts. Use 6–8 only when the chapter contains unusually many important developments. Do not fill the quota with minor facts.

Each fact should describe one durable piece of story knowledge in one short sentence.

Call each person, organization, place, and item by its name, spelled exactly as the chapter spells it. A title belongs to the person who holds it: never write a title as another person's name.

Write each fact about its main subject first: the person, organization, place, or item the fact is about starts the sentence.

Allowed categories:

* intro: who or what someone or something is, including role, origin, species, occupation, title, appearance, personality, purpose, or defining traits
* alias: another name, identity, disguise, codename, or hidden identity
* relationship: a meaningful bond or hierarchy between two characters
* ability: a skill, power, technique, capability, rank, level, or lasting change in what someone can do
* item: an important object, weapon, artifact, tool, resource, or possession, or who holds it
* affiliation: membership, allegiance, leadership, employment, rank, or position within an organization, or an organization's ties to another
* status: death, injury, imprisonment, disappearance, recovery, transformation, destruction, or another major condition change
* place: an important fact about a location, territory, settlement, building, or region
* event: another major development, discovery, decision, conflict, revelation, or world-building fact likely to matter later

An organization is any named group: a sect, clan, family house, school, guild, team, army, company, kingdom, or other faction.

For relationship facts, use a specific directional relationship word and write the sentence in this form:

<Character> is <relationship> to <Character>.

Use specific relationship words such as:
subordinate, superior, sibling, parent, child, mentor, student, enemy, ally, spouse, partner, rival, servant, master, friend

Do not use vague relationship descriptions such as:

* knows
* is connected to
* has a relationship with
* is associated with

Do not include:

* ordinary attacks, movement, travel, or conversation
* temporary emotions or reactions
* minor injuries or short-lived conditions
* routine use of an already-known skill, ability, or item
* facts already established earlier unless this chapter meaningfully changes them
* minor setting details with no likely future importance
* several facts describing the same development when they can be combined

After the facts, list every named organization, place, and item your facts mention, one per line, with its kind. Use the name exactly as it appears in your facts. Do not list people, techniques, titles, or unnamed things ("the city", "a sword").

Use exactly this output format:

## Facts

intro | <fact>
ability | <fact>
item | <fact>
relationship: <relationship> | <Character> is <relationship> to <Character>.
affiliation | <fact>
place | <fact>
event | <fact>

## Names

organization | <name>
place | <name>
item | <name>

Formatting rules:

* One fact or name per line.
* No bullets or numbering.
* No commentary before or after the sections.
* Do not add categories or kinds that are not listed above.
* Use `relationship: <relationship>` only for relationship facts.
* Relationship facts must name both characters and use the form `<Character> is <relationship> to <Character>`.
* If your facts name no organization, place, or item, leave the Names section empty.
* If there are no important facts, output exactly:

## Facts

None
